01 Assistive design

Wheelchair Grocery Transport System

A client at Calsted Independent Living needed a safer way to transport delivered groceries without interfering with wheelchair propulsion or requiring repeated rearward reaching. Our final system positioned the basket in front of the user while preserving normal wheelchair operation.

I led the initial client call and independently designed, laser cut, and installed the removable acrylic divider.

$147.53total cost
63%below budget
3 mmacrylic divider
Project memo ↗

The early concepts were much larger and more complicated. Client feedback made the real constraints obvious: weight, reach, strap tension, and access mattered more than adding mechanisms. The final design became simpler because we learned more.

02 Structural design

Acrylic Crown Truss

I designed and fabricated a 1000 mm acrylic truss, then built an Excel model to predict member forces, buckling, deflection, mass, and failure load. Testing smaller members helped me calibrate the model before the full structure went into the Instron.

Operations case study

Finding Louis

When my dog Louis went missing, I turned the search for him into a full-scale effort. I made and distributed flyers, knocked on doors, contacted local businesses, tracked leads in Excel, coordinated posts online, and eventually brought in a scent-trained bloodhound from out of state. It was personal, messy, and constantly changing, so keeping the information organized mattered.

  • 500+ weatherproofed flyers
  • 200+ doors knocked
  • 80 businesses contacted
